# Closing Entries Treatment Record

**Workflow:** Period-close process — close income/expense accounts to retained earnings
**Jurisdiction:** generic
**Scope:** Period-close process — close income/expense accounts to retained earnings

## Recognition timing

Closing entries are generated at the end of each accounting
period (monthly). Income and expense accounts are closed to
a retained earnings or equity account.

## Posting treatment

Revenue accounts: Dr Revenue, Cr Retained Earnings.
Expense accounts: Dr Retained Earnings, Cr Expense.
The net effect transfers net income to equity.

## Rounding

Closing entries use the exact account balances; no additional
rounding is applied.

## Reversal / void

Closing entries use the ADJUSTING transaction kind. They can
be reversed but NOT deleted (append-only model).

## Report inclusion

After closing, income/expense accounts show zero balance for
the period. The balance sheet reflects the updated equity.

## Assumptions

- Monthly close only.
- No year-end adjustments (e.g., depreciation, accruals).
- No audit trail for close entries beyond the standard
  audit log.

## Non-compliance disclaimer

Closing entries are automated and may NOT match your
jurisdiction's requirements for year-end procedures.
Consult your accountant for proper closing procedures.
