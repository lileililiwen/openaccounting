# Amortization Treatment Record

**Workflow:** Straight-line amortization of prepaid expenses and deferred revenue
**Jurisdiction:** generic
**Scope:** Straight-line amortization of prepaid expenses and deferred revenue

## Recognition timing

Amortization entries are generated monthly. Prepaid expenses
are amortized from the asset account to the expense account
over the service period. Deferred revenue is recognized from
the liability account to the revenue account over the service
period.

## Posting treatment

Prepaid amortization: Dr Expense, Cr Prepaid Asset.
Deferred revenue recognition: Dr Deferred Revenue (LIABILITY),
Cr Revenue (INCOME).

## Rounding

Monthly amounts are computed as total / number of months,
rounded to 2 decimal places. The final month absorbs any
rounding difference.

## Reversal / void

Amortization entries follow the standard append-only model.

## Report inclusion

Amortization expense appears in the income statement under
the appropriate expense category. Prepaid balances appear
on the balance sheet as current assets.

## Assumptions

- Straight-line method only.
- Monthly periods only; no partial-month proration.
- No amortization schedules with variable rates.

## Non-compliance disclaimer

Amortization calculations follow straight-line method only
and are NOT a substitute for professional accounting advice.
Complex amortization schedules (e.g., effective interest
method, units-of-production) require external tools.
